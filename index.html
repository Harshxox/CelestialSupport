What are the admission requirements for Heaven? ▼
Entry requirements vary by theological jurisdiction. Generally, we look at overall life conduct, intent, and compassion index. Please note that our admissions team operates independently of any single religious doctrine. All souls are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Appeals are available through Section 7 of the Celestial Charter.
Do I get to keep my memories? ▼
Yes! All memories are preserved and accessible. We also offer optional memory archiving for traumatic experiences. Our Celestial Memory Team provides complimentary sorting sessions every Tuesday (all days are technically Tuesday here, but you'll understand once you arrive).
Is the cloud comfortable? What are the sleeping arrangements? ▼
Heaven's clouds are rated 12-star (we invented our own rating system). Each soul receives a custom cloud allocation based on personal comfort preferences gathered at intake. You may upgrade your cloud at any time by filing a Class-A Comfort Request. Wings are standard issue but can be customized.
Can I visit living relatives? ▼
Inter-realm visitation is allowed under the Dream Protocol (Form DP-777). You may appear in the dreams of loved ones up to 3 times per earth-month. Poltergeist activity requires a separate permit which is rarely approved and usually results in a strongly worded letter from our legal department.
Is reincarnation available? ▼
Reincarnation is available as an optional upgrade package. The waiting list is currently 47 years (earth time). You may choose your preferred region, species, and general life difficulty setting. We cannot guarantee specific relationships or outcomes. Please read the full Reincarnation Terms & Conditions — it's 4.8 billion words long but our celestial legal team insists.
How does divine Wi-Fi work up here? ▼
Heaven operates on Omniscience Network™ — infinite bandwidth, zero latency, no password required (we know who you are). Earthly websites are accessible but social media is strongly discouraged. We maintain a curated celestial intranet with the complete archive of all human art, music, literature, and memes (yes, memes — they amused us greatly).
What if I was wrongly denied entry? ▼
The Celestial Appeals Court accepts all formal disputes. You will need Form CAC-404 (Soul Not Found) and three character references from people who knew you in life. Appeals are processed within one divine day, which is approximately 1,000 earth years. Expedited processing is available for an additional 50 Good Deeds on account.
